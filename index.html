Seeing Bias: auditing & repairing identity bias in museum image search

A study of a vision–language model (SigLIP-2) used to search 226k Harvard Art Museums objects — measuring how loaded words couple with identity, separating the model's bias from the collection's, and introducing a linear "de-coupler" that removes the coupling while keeping search intact. About

Art, math, and how machines see

I came to data science the long way — through painting and mathematics. A BA in mathematics and visual arts, an MFA, and years as an artist taught me to look carefully; graduate work in statistics and data science taught me to measure what I was looking at.

That pairing is my through-line. I'm drawn to how machines form concepts — how they see, abstract, and represent the world — and to where that meets creativity, interpretability, and, yes, bias. My work makes what these models learn visible and measurable, and, where it matters, shapeable.
